About us

About Dawat-e-Islami Canada

Dawat-e-Islami is a global, non-political Islamic organization devoted to spreading the Qur'an and Sunnah. It was founded on 2 September 1981 in Karachi, Pakistan, by Shaykh-e-Tariqat, Ameer-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat, Allama Maulana Muhammad Ilyas Attar Qadri Razavi.

Guided by compassion, Islamic knowledge and spiritual reform, it serves communities through Qur'an education, Islamic schools and universities, spiritual cure, welfare work and the Madani Channel. In Canada, this work reaches 6 provinces through 13 centres and two Jamia-tul-Madina campuses.

"I must strive to reform myself and the people of the entire world, In sha Allah."Our Madani Maqsad

Discover Islam

The five pillars of Islam

First pillar

Shahadah

Bearing witness that there is none worthy of worship except Allah, and that Muhammad ﷺ is His Messenger.
Second pillar

Salah

Worshipping Allah through the five daily prayers.
زَكَاة
Third pillar

Zakat

Giving a portion of eligible wealth each year to those entitled to it.
Fourth pillar

Sawm

Fasting from dawn to sunset through the month of Ramadan.
Fifth pillar

Hajj

Pilgrimage to Makkah once in a lifetime, for those who are able.
