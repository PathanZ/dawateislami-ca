Dawat-e-Islami was founded in 1981 in Karachi by Ameer-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat, Maulana Muhammad Ilyas Attar Qadri. Today it serves communities around the world through Qur'an education, Islamic scholarship and welfare work.

Faizan e Madina Vancouver is Dawat-e-Islami's centre for the Vancouver area, one of several Faizan e Madina centres across Canada.

Remember those who have passed away

Dua-e-Maghfirah

✦

Send us the names of your loved ones who have passed away. At the Weekly Ijtima on Thursday, the whole gathering will make a collective dua for their forgiveness and mercy.

Why make dua for the deceased

  • رَبَّنَا اغْفِرْ لَنَا وَلِإِخْوَانِنَا الَّذِينَ سَبَقُونَا بِالْإِيمَانِ

    "Our Lord, forgive us and our brothers who came before us in faith." The Qur'an teaches the believers to pray for those who have gone before them.

    Surah Al-Hashr, 59:10

  • When a person dies, their deeds come to an end except three: ongoing charity, beneficial knowledge, and a righteous child who prays for them. Your dua keeps reaching them.

    Sahih Muslim, 1631

  • A person's rank in Paradise is raised, and they ask how. They are told it is because their child sought forgiveness for them.

    Sunan Ibn Majah, 3660

  • When you pray for a fellow Muslim in their absence, an angel says, "Ameen, and the same for you." Making dua for others brings blessings back to you too.

    Sahih Muslim, 2733
How it works
  • Only 3 names are read each Thursday. Once 3 names are in, that Thursday is full. First come, first served.
  • You can book for this Thursday or the next one, up to 2 weeks ahead.
  • Names for this Thursday are accepted until 5:00 PM that day.
