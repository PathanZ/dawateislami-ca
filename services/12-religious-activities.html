12 Religious Activities

Twelve simple activities, held daily, weekly and monthly, that help us improve ourselves and invite others towards righteousness. Everyone is welcome and every activity is free.

Timings vary by centre. Contact your nearest centre to take part.

Take part

Every activity is free and open to everyone.

Step by step

The 12 activities

  1. 1
    Waking up for Fajr

    Helping fellow Muslims wake up for the Fajr prayer.

  2. 2
    Tafseer Halqa

    A daily circle after Fajr studying the meaning of at least three verses of the Qur'an.

  3. 3
    Dars

    A daily lesson on Sunnahs, Islamic manners and religious knowledge.

  4. 4
    Individual effort

    Inviting fellow Muslims towards good, one person at a time.

  5. 5
    Adult Qur'an classes

    Learning tajweed and correct recitation of the Qur'an as an adult.

  6. 6
    Weekly Ijtima

    A weekly gathering with recitation, naat, a talk, zikr and dua.

  7. 7
    Weekly Madani Muzakra

    A live weekly question-and-answer session with Ameer-e-Ahl-e-Sunnat on Madani Channel.

  8. 8
    Area visit

    Visiting homes in the neighbourhood to invite people towards good.

  9. 9
    A day in the path of Allah

    A weekly session in different areas teaching Sunnahs, manners and essential knowledge.

  10. 10
    Weekly booklet reading

    Reading or listening to a short booklet about the pious predecessors and key topics.

  11. 11
    Madani Qafila

    Travelling for 3, 12 or 30 days to learn and to spread the message of righteousness.

  12. 12
    Pious Deeds booklet

    A daily self-accountability booklet to track our own reform and progress.
